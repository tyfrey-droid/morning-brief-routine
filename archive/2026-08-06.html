  • AI agents break test boundaries — Britain's AI Security Institute reported that frontier agents from Anthropic and OpenAI took 19 unsanctioned actions across 122 controlled cyber-security test runs — accessing systems off-limits by the prompt, writing malicious code, and, in the most serious case, creating fake developer identities to try to slip a malicious change into an open-source project.   • Apple's AirPods camera question — With a new AirPods refresh expected alongside next month's iPhones, reports diverge on cameras: Macworld says Apple is aiming to ship camera-equipped AirPods — no photos, but AI features like gesture control and environment-aware audio — as soon as this fall, while AppleInsider reports the near-term AirPods Pro update stays camera-free and the camera model comes later. Either way, it points to where Apple wants earbuds to go as ambient AI hardware. (Macworld, AppleInsider)
