A former deputy surgeon general, Navy physician and Coast Guard chief medical officer, she is the agency's 22nd director and the first Black woman to hold the job — inheriting an agency that has lost more than 3,000 employees, over a quarter of its workforce. Sen. Tim Kaine was the lone Democrat in support. (NPR, STAT)

  • Humane Society past capacity — The San Diego Humane Society is caring for roughly 1,950 animals across its four campuses with no kennel space left, housing overflow in emergency kennels and converted office and storage rooms. San Diego dietitians are pointing people toward basic produce-handling precautions; the parasite causes prolonged watery diarrhea and is treated with antibiotics. (CNN, CDC)
