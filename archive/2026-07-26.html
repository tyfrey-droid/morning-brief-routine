🌍 World
  • Deadly van attack at Berlin Pride — A van drove into crowds at Berlin's Christopher Street Day celebrations near Tiergarten on Saturday night, killing one woman and injuring at least 16, several critically, before the driver fled on foot. Police halted the festival and on Sunday issued a wanted notice for a 21-year-old suspect; a motive has not been confirmed. (CNN, Al Jazeera, Washington Post)
  • DR Congo Ebola deaths pass 1,300 — The death toll from the Democratic Republic of Congo's Ebola outbreak, driven by the rarer Bundibugyo strain, has climbed past 1,300 with confirmed cases above 3,000 — now the fastest-spreading outbreak on record, up more than 40% in five days. Ituri province accounts for nearly 90% of cases, and there is no approved vaccine or treatment for this strain. (Al Jazeera, WHO)
  • Cargo ship sinks in South China Sea — The Vietnam-flagged vessel Khoi Nguyen 18, carrying 62 people, sank in bad weather near Fiery Cross Reef late Saturday. By Sunday midday 39 crew had been rescued — largely by Chinese vessels and a helicopter working alongside a Vietnamese ship — with 23 still missing as a multinational search continued in a disputed stretch of the sea. (The Japan Times, Free Malaysia Today)
🇺🇸 US & Politics
  • Court blocks Trump mail-voting order — The Boston-based 1st U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals refused (2–1) on Saturday to let the administration enforce President Trump's executive order tightening mail-in voting rules across 23 states, leaving in place a lower court's finding that key parts are unconstitutional. The suit was led by California, Massachusetts, Nevada and Washington ahead of November's midterms. (CNBC, U.S. News)
  • Trump returns to correspondents' dinner — President Trump attended the rescheduled White House Correspondents' Association Dinner on Friday, held at the Waldorf Astoria three months after the original April event ended abruptly when a gunman fired shots at the Washington Hilton. In his remarks he mocked the press, Joe Biden and members of his own cabinet, drawing criticism over the tone toward the press corps. (NPR, CNN, Axios)
