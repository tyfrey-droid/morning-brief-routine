🧬 Mito Research

  • Zagociguat has failed in MELAS and been dropped — This is the hardest kind of news to report plainly, so: Tisento Therapeutics has told the MELAS community it is ending development of zagociguat for MELAS and other primary mitochondrial diseases. The company says its Phase 2b PRIZM trial — 43 patients, two 12-week treatment periods either side of a 4-week washout — did not meet any primary or secondary endpoint, and that no benefit over placebo appeared in any predefined subgroup. No safety signals were found. The open-label extension is being wound down and study teams have contacted participants. Zagociguat was the furthest-advanced MELAS-specific drug candidate, an sGC stimulator with FDA Fast Track designation since June 2025, and topline data had been expected this quarter; the decision came instead. What this changes practically: the near-term pipeline for m.3243A>G now rests on other mechanisms rather than this one, and the result is a company statement, with no peer-reviewed publication of the data yet. (Tisento Therapeutics, ClinicalTrials.gov) ⚠️ industry statement, not peer-reviewed
