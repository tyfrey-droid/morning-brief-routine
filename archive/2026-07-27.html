🌍 World
  • US and Iran pause strikes — After nearly two weeks of nightly US bombing and Iranian retaliation on Gulf hosts, the US held off attacking Iran for a second straight day Sunday and Tehran said it did the same, opening space for interim-ceasefire talks reportedly centered on eased Strait of Hormuz transit rules. Netanyahu is due at the White House Tuesday for his seventh meeting with Trump since the war began, though Trump has kept the option of renewed strikes open. (NPR, CNN, Al Jazeera)
  • France wildfire eases at last — The vast Gironde-region blaze that forced roughly 250,000 people from their homes across France and Spain — France's largest evacuation since World War II — slowed overnight into Monday, with no new evacuation orders as some 2,500 firefighters, soldiers and aircraft held the fire short of Bordeaux. Blazes in central Spain are also coming under control, and President Macron pledged state help to rebuild the ravaged areas. (NPR, France 24, CNN)
🇺🇸 US & Politics
  • Shooting kills three in Seattle — Three people were killed and four wounded — including a toddler — Sunday evening at the Bite of Seattle food festival at Seattle Center near the Space Needle, in what police describe as a shootout between two gunmen. One suspect surrendered and was taken into custody and two guns were recovered; police continued searching for a second shooter. (CNN, NBC News, CBS News)
  • Democrats unite against war funding — Congressional Democrats have coalesced in near-total opposition to the GOP's roughly $95 billion Iran-war-and-priorities package, which passed the House 216–214, and are demanding a troop withdrawal — a rare unified wartime funding revolt against a sitting president. Party leaders argue the money should instead go toward lowering costs at home, making the Iran operation among the most partisan US military campaigns in modern history. (CNN, NPR, PBS NewsHour)
⚡ Tech & AI
  • iOS 26.6 arrives today — Apple is set to release iOS 26.6 today, alongside iPadOS 26.6, macOS Tahoe 26.6 and companion updates, following the release candidate seeded July 20. It's mainly a maintenance update — bug fixes, security patches, a CarPlay fix and a higher 3,000-contact block limit — but it quietly begins building the on-device semantic Spotlight index that iOS 27's revamped Siri will rely on, so installing now spares upgraders a later re-indexing wait. (9to5Mac, Forbes)
  • Apple Music prices go up — Apple raised US prices across Apple Music and Apple One for the first time since 2022. Apple Music Individual moved from $10.99 to $11.99 a month, Family from $16.99 to $19.99 and Student from $5.99 to $6.99, with Apple citing rising licensing costs paid to rights holders; several international markets saw increases too. (MacRumors, 9to5Mac)
  • AMD to invest up to $5B in Anthropic — AMD agreed to invest as much as $5 billion in Anthropic as part of a multi-gigawatt chip deal under which Anthropic will buy up to 2 gigawatts of AMD's next-generation Instinct MI450 GPUs starting in the first half of 2027. AMD's equity stake is tied to deployment milestones — the latest of the "circular" AI-infrastructure deals in which a chipmaker invests in a major customer. (CNBC, Bloomberg)
  • OpenAI launches Presence — OpenAI introduced Presence, an enterprise platform for deploying voice and chat AI agents with built-in guardrails, permission controls, escalation-to-human rules and pre-deployment simulations, where a Codex-powered system reviews interactions and proposes fixes staff must approve. Early customers include BBVA Mexico and SoftBank; OpenAI says Presence already handles its own English phone support, resolving about 75% of issues without a human. (OpenAI, VentureBeat)

  • US–Mexico desalination deal — Mexico plans to build an $840 million desalination plant in Rosarito to turn ocean water into freshwater, with the US helping pipe supply to cities that need it, as part of a plan to reduce Mexico's draw from the Colorado River. Construction is slated to break ground August 1, though the Sheinbaum administration has struggled to line up a private builder. (Voice of San Diego, Water Education Foundation)

The sale is expected to close by July 31. (Times of San Diego)
  • Community Power CEO review surfaces — Public records show employees of San Diego Community Power — California's second-largest community-choice energy agency, with a roughly $1.2 billion budget — gave CEO Karin Burns middling-to-good marks in an April staff survey. The review came before Burns went on unexplained leave in June. (Voice of San Diego)
  • Gas prices climb 12th straight day — The average price of a gallon of regular unleaded in San Diego County rose again to about $5.72, its 12th consecutive daily increase, tracking a broader statewide climb. (Times of San Diego)
