🧬 Mito Research
  • Positive Phase 3 in Leigh syndrome — A Japanese Phase 3 randomized-withdrawal trial of SPP-004 (5-aminolevulinic acid plus sodium ferrous citrate) reported that among young Leigh syndrome patients who had responded during an open-label lead-in, far fewer relapsed on the drug than on placebo — 15.4% versus 50.0% discontinued for inadequate efficacy at 48 weeks — with only mild side effects. It is one of the first positive controlled efficacy signals for a small-molecule metabolic therapy in a primary mitochondrial disease, and the compound acts on the heme/oxidative-phosphorylation machinery impaired in MELAS too — though the trial studied Leigh syndrome, not MELAS. ⚠️ Industry-funded (sponsor SBI Pharmaceuticals). (PLOS One)
