🧬 Mito Research
  • L-citrulline shows an early safety signal in MELAS — A first-in-MELAS Phase 1 dose-finding trial (10 adults) tested L-citrulline to correct the nitric-oxide deficiency thought to drive the stroke-like episodes that define MELAS. No dose-limiting toxicities were seen up to 40 g, and 7 of 10 patients showed increased cerebral blood flow — strongest in the occipital region, where MELAS strokes cluster — though higher doses raised guanidino-compound outliers that need follow-up. Why it matters: it moves an accessible amino-acid supplement, aimed at the vascular mechanism of stroke-like episodes, onto a formal dosing and safety footing, though the data are adult-only, small, and preliminary, and efficacy still needs a randomized placebo-controlled trial. (Molecular Genetics and Metabolism)
