  • Navi Pillay wins the Nobel Peace Prize — The Norwegian Nobel Committee awarded the 2026 peace prize in Oslo this morning to Navanethem “Navi” Pillay, the South African jurist and former UN human rights chief, for work the committee said helped ensure war crimes, crimes against humanity and genocide are actually prosecuted. Pillay was part of the anti-apartheid movement, judged at the International Criminal Tribunal for Rwanda, and until recently chaired the UN commission of inquiry that concluded Israel committed genocide in Gaza — a line on her résumé that makes the award contentious in Israel. Committee chair Jørgen Watne Frydnes framed the choice around the rule of law; the committee could not reach Pillay beforehand, and she learned of it at a forum in Nuremberg. The ceremony is December 10. (Al Jazeera, CNN, NobelPrize.org)

🔋 Electric Vehicles
  • VW tells ID.4 owners to stop at 80% — Volkswagen is recalling 22,524 ID.4s from the 2023–2026 model years in the US because charging the high-voltage battery above 80% can trigger thermal propagation — a fire inside the pack. The root cause has not been identified; the cells come from SK Battery America, and VW estimates 0.1% of the recalled cars are actually affected, after 22 related reports or complaints between January 2024 and August 2026. Until a fix exists the interim remedy is to charge no higher than 80%, which some cars will enforce in software, with dashboard warnings and in some cases stickers. Owner letters go out November 27. This is the third ID.4 battery campaign in this period. (InsideEVs, electrive, Kelley Blue Book)
  • California will fund a whole charging port (general EV news) — A new California Energy Commission window opened this week that can cover up to 100% of eligible installation costs for public DC fast chargers, with per-port caps of $55,000 for 150–275 kW stalls and $100,000 for 275 kW and above. Roughly $28 million is in this round, which runs through January 14, 2027; projects must be shovel-ready with permits issued, publicly accessible, and at least half CCS connectors. (InsideEVs, electrive)
