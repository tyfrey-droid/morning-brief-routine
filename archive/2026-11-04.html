⚡ Tech & AI

  • WORKFLOWKeynote, Pages and Numbers get an assistant — and an agent door — Apple is adding AI assistants to Logic Pro, Keynote, Pages and Numbers, announced October 27 and due on the App Store this month as part of Creator Studio. Each assistant reads the open document: Pages drafts and restructures text, Numbers builds calculations and suggests chart types, Keynote handles slide design. The more useful part for a one-person shop is that the three iWork apps now support MCP, so an outside agent can read and edit a proposal or a budget directly. Caveats are real — beta, US English only, macOS 27 or later on an Apple Intelligence-capable machine, and the AI features sit behind the Creator Studio subscription even though the apps stay free. (Thurrott, heise online)
  • Apple Intelligence reaches China on Alibaba's model — Apple and Alibaba confirmed on October 31 that Qwen will power Apple Intelligence in mainland China, and the features went into the developer beta for testing today ahead of a broader December release with iOS 27.3. Qwen handles requests inside Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence; the rebuilt Siri shown at WWDC is not part of the China launch. Users are prompted for permission before a question, document or image is sent to Qwen, and Alibaba says those requests will not be used to train its models. (Computerworld, Asia News Network)

🌊 San Diego

  • Measure B is too close to call — The countywide half-cent sales-tax increase was sitting on a knife edge in the first returns, with Yes at about 50.3 per cent to No at 49.7 — a margin of a few thousand votes out of more than 650,000 counted. Backers estimate it would raise roughly $400 million a year; it would take the rate in the City of San Diego from 7.75 to 8.25 per cent.
