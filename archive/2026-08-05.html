🌍 World
  • Hormuz reopening deal nears — President Trump said a US-backed agreement to reopen the Strait of Hormuz could be announced as soon as Wednesday, with Iran and Oman closing on an interim 60-day arrangement that would give Tehran a managed role over one traffic lane while restoring free navigation. The deal is meant to cement the US–Iran ceasefire and revive nuclear talks; the strait normally carries roughly a fifth of the world's seaborne oil and gas, and its closure had jolted energy prices. (NBC News, Axios)
  • Netanyahu rejects Gaza withdrawal — In a rare public break with Washington, Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said Israel "did not agree" to the US-backed Board of Peace plan to disarm Hamas and will not pull back from the roughly 60% of Gaza it holds until Hamas is "completely disarmed," calling the draft "not our draft." His pushback casts fresh doubt on the phased deal, under which Hamas would begin disarming as Israel halted strikes and withdrew. (Washington Post, Al Jazeera)
  • Record heat, wildfires grip Europe — A fourth heat wave since May pushed temperatures past 40°C across France, Spain and Greece, driving record wildfire destruction and a drought that has lowered the Rhine and Danube and stirred worries at nuclear plants. Fires easing near Athens on Tuesday had forced more than 1,000 evacuations, Spain's Ávila blaze became the largest in its recorded history, and France set a national record for area burned this year. (NPR, France 24)
