🇺🇸 US & Politics

  • Peltola takes the last Senate seat — The Associated Press called Alaska's Senate race on Tuesday evening for Democrat Mary Peltola over incumbent Republican Dan Sullivan; she took a majority of first-choice votes, so no ranked-choice round was needed. It was the final midterm Senate race to be settled and gives Democrats 56 seats from January after nine flips this cycle. Peltola will be the first Alaska Native to serve in the Senate and the first Democrat to win a Senate race in the state since 2008. The contest was complicated by a spoiler candidate who shares the incumbent's name, and some Republicans have suggested trying to block her from being seated. (NPR, CNN, CNBC)
  • The FAA stops installing the routers — The agency confirmed Tuesday that it has halted installation of the new routers behind Friday's nearly five-hour nationwide ground stop. The FAA says the system was overwhelmed by roughly 1,000 connection attempts every four to five seconds after a router went in, disabling radar and communications feeds at 17 of the country's 22 regional control centres. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy told lawmakers “you will have more of these” without a rebuild and raised his ask to $50 billion; Senate Commerce Democrats wrote to Duffy and FAA Administrator Bryan Bedford demanding an explanation and advance briefings before future changes, noting the figure is $20 billion more than the request their own department and the White House budget office had sent days earlier. (Axios, CNBC, ABC News)
